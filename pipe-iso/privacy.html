This app does not display ads. It contains no advertising SDK, and it does not collect, use, or share an advertising identifier (AAID). Earlier versions of the app did show ads through Google AdMob; that has been removed.

2. Analytics and crash reporting

To keep the app stable, we use Google’s Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports) and Google Analytics for Firebase (anonymous usage statistics). These process technical information such as device model, operating system version, app version, error details, and screen navigation. They do not process information that directly identifies you, and this information is not used for advertising. See Firebase privacy information and Google’s Privacy Policy.

3. In-app purchases

The precision calculation menus (elbow miter, 2D offset, ISO 3D) can be opened free of charge up to 3 times per day; the allowance refills at midnight on your device. The app offers a one-time “Pro” purchase that removes this daily limit and the PDF watermark. It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. Purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment-card details; we only receive confirmation of whether the purchase was completed. The app stores only a purchased / not-purchased flag and the daily count on your device.

4. Feedback

If you use the in-app “Send feedback” option, your own email app opens and you decide what to send. We receive only what is in that email (including your email address) and use it solely to reply and improve the app.

5. Children

This app is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13.

6. Your rights

We do not hold personal data on our own servers; the data described above is processed by Google. For requests about data handled by Google’s services, please refer to Google’s Privacy Policy. Users in the EEA/UK have the right to access, correct, delete, and restrict processing of their personal data, and to withdraw consent. For any questions, contact gua091016@gmail.com.

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be shown on this page with an updated date.
