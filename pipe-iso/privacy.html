This policy covers the Android app and its introduction website. No separate app account is required. We do not ask for your name or email to use the calculator. Technical data is processed by the Google services described below; if you contact us, we also receive the information you choose to send.

1. Sketches, calculations and local storage

Sketches, calculation inputs, notes, language and unit settings, fitting/connection settings, saved projects, Pro entitlement cache and weekly usage counts are stored locally on your device. The app does not operate a developer-hosted sketch storage or automatic sketch-upload service. Operating-system backup or device-transfer services may copy app data according to your device and account settings.

Clearing app storage or uninstalling can remove local data. Exported or shared files, files saved outside app storage, and operating-system backups must be managed separately. Clearing app data or uninstalling does not cancel a Google Play subscription.

2. Advertising

The current app does not display ads and does not include an advertising SDK. Android advertising-ID permissions are removed, and advertising-ID collection by Firebase Analytics is disabled. This does not disable the separate installation identifiers used for analytics and crash reporting.

3. Analytics and crash reporting

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand app use and Firebase Crashlytics to diagnose errors. These services process app/installation identifiers, device and operating-system information, app version, usage/session events, crash information and stack traces. Analytics may also process in-app purchase events and approximate location derived from network information. This is not GPS location collection; the app does not request location permission.

Installation identifiers can distinguish installations, so these reports should not be described as completely anonymous. We do not intentionally attach your sketch or note contents, name or email address to analytics events. Error reports can include diagnostic information associated with an error. Google processes this information, and the developer can review reports in the service consoles to improve reliability and usability.

Google may process data in countries outside your country of residence. Retention depends on the service and its settings; Firebase currently describes a 90-day retention period for Crashlytics traces and associated identifiers before removal begins. See Firebase privacy information, Analytics data collection and Google’s Privacy Policy for details.

4. Google Play purchases and subscriptions

Elbow miter calculations include five free uses per week. All 2D offset shapes share a separate allowance of five calculations per week. PDF and DXF exports share another allowance of five exports per week. Allowances reset every Monday at 00:00 in the device’s local time, with no rollover. Creating/editing sketches, browsing menus and looking up fitting dimensions do not use an allowance. Free and Pro PDF exports have no watermark.

New Pro purchases are monthly or yearly auto-renewing subscriptions, providing unlimited elbow miter calculations, 2D offset calculations and PDF/DXF exports. The local price and billing period appear in Google Play before purchase. Cancel through Google Play; access remains through the paid period. Existing lifetime owners retain Pro without additional payment and can restore ownership using the original Google Play account.

Google Play processes payment and provides product, purchase-state and ownership information to the app. We do not receive your payment-card details. The app caches lifetime ownership, subscription product, the last successful ownership-check time and weekly usage counts locally. If the store is unavailable, the last successful subscription check may be used for up to 24 hours. The app does not send receipts to a separate developer verification server.

5. File exports, sharing and support

PDF/DXF files and saved sketches can contain your drawing, dimensions, notes and project information. Exporting or opening a share sheet does not automatically send them to the developer. When you choose a sharing destination, the selected app or service processes the files under its own policy.

The feedback button opens a draft in your email app. It includes app version, platform, selected language and Pro status; you can edit these before sending. If you send it, we receive your sender address, message and any attachments you include. We use them to answer and investigate your request, retain them only as needed for support or applicable obligations, and accept deletion requests at the contact address above. Do not include confidential drawings unless needed for your request.

6. Introduction website

The website stores your selected display language in browser local storage. You can remove this by clearing site data. The introduction page does not embed Firebase Analytics, advertising trackers or a contact form. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub processes access information such as IP addresses and request details to deliver and protect the site. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. External links, including Google Play, are governed by the destination’s policy.

7. Your choices and rights

Contact us for questions or requests about data processed for this app, including access, correction or deletion where applicable. Local data can be removed through device settings; browser language data can be removed through browser settings. Deleting local data does not itself delete already transmitted Firebase reports or previously sent emails. We assess service-side requests using the information available and the relevant provider’s tools; we may be unable to identify a particular report without sufficient information. Applicable law may also provide rights to restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, or complain to a data protection authority.

8. Children and policy changes

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We update this page and its date when the policy changes. Contact us if you believe a child has sent personal information.
