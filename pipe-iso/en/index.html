Pipe ISO Calculator is a pipe fitter app for site and shop work: draw the line on an isometric grid, enter the CL dimensions, and the fitting take-offs are subtracted to give the CUT length of each pipe. It also covers 45° and rolling offsets, parallel pipe offsets and elbow miter cuts.

Main features

Pipe cut length (CUT)

Enter CL dimensions and get CUT lengths with ASME B16.9 butt-weld and B16.11 socket-weld/threaded take-offs, up to 24".

Piping isometric sketch

Draw on an ISO grid, add elbows, tees, reducers, flanges, valves and olets, and check welds and the final cut list.

Offsets and miters

45°, rolling and 90°+45° offsets, parallel pipe offsets for 2–4 pipes, and elbow miter cutting.

Where it helps

Working out take-offs by hand on a crowded site is slow and easy to get wrong. Draw the line once, and the app keeps every CL and CUT dimension together.

Share the sketch and cut list as a PDF with the shop, foreman or client, or export DXF to open the drawing in CAD. Units can be mm or feet-inch with 1/16" fractions.
