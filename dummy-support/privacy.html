  • 새들 컷 계산, 전개선 계산, PDF 생성은 사용자의 기기에서 처리됩니다.

Summary: Dummy Support Calculator itself does not collect direct identifiers such as your name, email address, phone number, or location. Calculations and PDF generation are processed on your device. 1. Information We Collect

The app itself does not collect personally identifiable information such as name, email address, phone number, or precise location.

2. How Data Is Processed

  • Saddle-cut calculations, development calculations, and PDF generation are processed on your device.
  • Core calculation features can work without an internet connection.
  • A network connection is used only for in-app purchases and restores, Play in-app update checks, and sending diagnostic reports.

3. Data Sharing

When you use the share feature to export a generated PDF or image to another app, that action is initiated by you. The app does not store those files on a separate server.

Diagnostic information may be sent to Google (Firebase) as described in Section 5.

4. Advertising and In-App Purchases

This app does not display ads. It contains no advertising SDK and does not collect, use, or share an advertising ID (AAID).

The free plan allows up to 3 calculation runs per day, and the allowance refills at local midnight. A one-time in-app purchase (Pro) removes the daily limit.

  • Payment processing and purchase records are handled by Google Play. The app does not collect or store payment method details such as card numbers.
  • The app stores only a purchased / not purchased flag on your device so entitlement works offline.
  • Usage counts are stored on your device only and are not transmitted anywhere.
  • Google Play privacy information: policies.google.com/privacy

5. Analytics and Diagnostics

To improve stability, the app uses Google's Firebase Crashlytics (crash reporting) and Google Analytics for Firebase (anonymous usage statistics). This includes anonymous, aggregated information such as device model, OS version, app version, error details, and screen navigation. It does not include information that directly identifies you.

  • Firebase privacy information: firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • This information is not used for advertising and is not shared with advertising providers.

6. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Retention and Deletion

Values and settings stored on the device can be deleted by uninstalling the app, clearing app data, or using an in-app reset feature if available.
