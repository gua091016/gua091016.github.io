Boilermaker Calc Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-28 · App: Boilermaker Calc (com.gua091016.boilermaker)

1. Information we collect

The app does not collect your name, contacts, precise location or advertising ID. The dimensions you enter and the results never leave your phone. There are no ads.

To fix bugs and improve the app, only the following is sent to Google Firebase:

  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics): the error log when the app crashes, device model, Android version, app version, and a random per-install identifier.
  • Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase): which tool screens are opened, app opens and session length, device model, Android version, language, country (approximate region), and an app instance ID.

This data is not used to identify you, is never sold, and is not used for advertising. Google processes it on our behalf under the Firebase privacy terms. Crash reports are kept for 90 days and analytics data for 2 months.

2. Stored on your device only

  • Unit setting (mm / inch), language, free-trial start time, weekly free-use count and the tools opened today, Pro purchase / subscription status.
  • These stay on your phone and are removed when the app is uninstalled.

3. Payments

Pro monthly and yearly subscriptions are handled through Google Play Billing; they renew automatically and can be cancelled anytime in Google Play. Payment details are handled by Google; the developer never receives card information. See the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Feedback

"Send feedback" opens your email app. Emails you choose to send are used only to reply and improve the app, and are never shared.

5. Exported files

PDF and DXF files are created on your phone and shared only with apps you choose.

This app is a tool for tradespeople and is not directed at children.
